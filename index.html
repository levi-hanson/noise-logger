Settings

The offset is added to the mic's raw level to approximate real-world dB. Compare against a phone sound-meter app and adjust; it re-scales all stored days. A loud event is a level above the rolling median by the margin, for at least the minimum duration.

Levels only: the microphone signal is reduced to one loudness number every 100 ms inside the browser. No audio is saved or sent anywhere. The numbers are stored only in this browser on this computer. Keep this tab open and the laptop awake while logging.
